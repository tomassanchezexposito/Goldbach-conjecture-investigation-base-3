## Immutable mathematics and proof status

For every even integer `N>=4`, the author's cases are `N=p+1`, `N=2p`, and `N=p+q` with distinct primes `p<q`. Define `H={N even>=4: N-1 and N/2 composite}`, `H1={N even>=8: N-1 prime and N/2 composite}`, and `R(N)=#{p<q genuine primes: p+q=N}`. Strong Goldbach allows the diagonal. The equivalence of the author's universal assertion with `R>0` on `H` is **not** equivalence with strong Goldbach; Goldbach requires `R>0` on `H union H1` (or a prime midpoint). The signed Type II theorem required for genuine primes remains **OPEN**. The deletion-stable-input route has a restricted **NO-GO**.

**No universal proof, no postulated real-prime signed margin, and no independent human acceptance of T73.5/T74.3/T75.1 are represented in this ledger.**
