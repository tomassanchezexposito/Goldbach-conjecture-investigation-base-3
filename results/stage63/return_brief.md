# Stage 63 return brief

We completed and checkpointed the Stage 63 research program on its dedicated branch. The work proves exact counting identities for shared prime positions, wheel-210 residue factors, and the target-specific Fourier coefficient. It also verifies several proposed moment and graph criteria and proves a conditional result: a sufficiently strong dyadic mean-square estimate would rule out every sufficiently large residual exception.

That mean-square estimate has not been proved, and the reviewed published average and exceptional-set results do not give a bound for every target. An independent finite check found representations for all 258 residual even numbers through 1000, but finite checks do not prove universal coverage.

The original three-case conjecture remains open. No proof of Goldbach is claimed. The next research objective is a precise audit of whether known unconditional circle-method estimates can provide the missing pointwise signed bound, while retaining the exact residual quantifier.
