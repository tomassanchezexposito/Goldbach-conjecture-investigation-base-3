# Stage 65 — Independent adversarial review

## Findings audited

1. **Sum/difference correction.** Direct expansion confirms that $S_w^2$ has frequency $p+q$ and $|S_w|^2$ has frequency $p-q$. The exact N=8 case and the full 16-frequency regression pass using independent direct and finite-field DFT methods. The fourth-moment integrand remains $|S_w|^4$.
2. **Kernel identities.** The shift multiplier has sign $e(-h\alpha)$ under $\widehat f(n)=\int f(\alpha)e(-n\alpha)d\alpha$. Orthogonality gives full-circle $\|K_\lambda\|_2^2=\sum_h|\lambda_h|^2$. Restriction to the minor arcs gives an inequality, not a Parseval equality. Rational Python and finite-field SageMath checks agree on the separate finite identities.
3. **Fejér constants.** For $\omega_h=(H+1-|h|)/(H+1)^2$, direct finite summation yields coefficient-weight sum one, squared norm $(2H^2+4H+3)/(3(H+1)^3)$, and first absolute moment $H(H+2)/(3(H+1))$. The associated multiplier's circle integral is $\omega_0=1/(H+1)$, not one. This distinction is explicit in the corrected kernel report. The displayed conditional criterion uses the exact constants.
4. **Logical scope.** The isolated negative coefficient is only an abstract sequence satisfying a global norm budget; it is not claimed to be generated by primes. Hence it refutes a deduction from the norm hypothesis alone, not a prime-theoretic inequality.
5. **Residual arithmetic.** For even $N>4$, the prime 2 cannot occur in a representation; for $N=4$, $2+2$ is the excluded diagonal. On $H$, $N/2$ composite excludes the equal-prime diagonal. The wheel count is a CRT local-admissibility count only. At 210 and 420 it is 48 for each; their common odd singular-series multiplier is $16/5$, but only 210 lies in $H$.
6. **Anchors.** A shared-prime correlation at a target is not a lower bound unless the required representation exists. An argument selecting an anchor representation at the target under proof is circular. No non-circular lower bound for the genuine-prime signed four-position contribution was found.

## Counterexample and scope audit

- The exact polynomial $S=e(3\alpha)+e(5\alpha)$ refutes the invalid substitution between sum and difference coefficients.
- A one-coordinate vector $c(N)=-A$, $c(n)=0$ for $n\ne N$, refutes inversion of an attenuated normalized shift average using only a global $\ell^2$ budget, whenever $A^2$ is within that budget.
- The pair 210, 420 refutes the claim that wheel or full singular-series values alone determine residual-set membership.
- None of these is a counterexample to Goldbach or to a stronger theorem with additional arithmetic hypotheses.

This review establishes the displayed finite algebra and checks the stated theorem hypotheses against the inherited dated source audit.
