## 1. Precise inherited mistake (do not silently rewrite Stage 68)

The Stage 68 document \`results/stage68/02_exact_budget_optimization.md\` considered
\[
G_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
G_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad B_1=B_2=1.
\]
Its correct original rank-one optimum is
\[
U_{\mathrm{rank1}}=\max_{\|z\|_2=1}\bigl(\sqrt{z^*G_1z}+\sqrt{z^*G_2z}\bigr)
=\max_{|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1}(|z_1|+|z_2|)=\sqrt2.
\]
However it falsely asserted that replacing \(zz^*\) by **all** trace-one positive semidefinite matrices gives optimum \(2\). In fact, for \(X\succeq0,\operatorname{tr}X=1\), let \(t=X_{11}\in[0,1]\). Then \(X_{22}=1-t\) and
\[
U_{\mathrm{PSD}}=\max_{0\le t\le1}(\sqrt{t}+\sqrt{1-t})
=\sqrt2
\]
by Cauchy–Schwarz, attained at \(X=I_2/2\). There is **NO SDP-lifting gap** in this two-projector example. The number \(2\) is merely the separate **triangle upper bound**
\(\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(G_1)}+\sqrt{\lambda_{\max}(G_2)}=2\).

**First invalid inference:** substituting the triangle upper bound \(2\) for the optimum of the relaxed trace-one PSD problem. The inherited \`results/stage68/verify_stage68.py\` is incapable of detecting this because it asserts a tautological identity \(\sqrt{1/2}+\sqrt{1/2}-2=\sqrt2-2\), not an optimization condition. Its report/critic consequently mislabels a triangle gap as an SDP gap. Preserve these historical source files and record the correction in the new Stage 69 branch.

## 2. Theorem 69.1 — a genuine strict PSD-lifting gap in an abstract 2×2 Hermitian example (PROVED)

Let \(\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\sigma_z\) be the Pauli matrices
\[
\sigma_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
\sigma_y=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
\sigma_z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For \(a\in\{x,y,z\}\) and \(\varepsilon\in\{+1,-1\}\), define six rank-one Hermitian projectors
\[
P_{a,\varepsilon}=\tfrac12(I_2+\varepsilon\sigma_a).
\]
Each \(P_{a,\varepsilon}\succeq0\) has eigenvalues \(0,1\). Give all six terms budget \(B_{a,\varepsilon}=1\), and consider
\[
\mathcal F(X)=\sum_{a\in\{x,y,z\}}\sum_{\varepsilon=\pm1}
\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(P_{a,\varepsilon}X)},
\quad X\succeq0,\quad \operatorname{tr}X=1.
\]

**Mixed-state optimum:** For every axis \(a\), the two nonnegative scalar expectations satisfy
\(\operatorname{tr}(P_{a,+}X)+\operatorname{tr}(P_{a,-}X)=1\), so Cauchy–Schwarz yields
\[
\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(P_{a,+}X)}
+\sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(P_{a,-}X)}\le\sqrt2.
\]
At \(X=I_2/2\), both expectations are \(1/2\) for every axis, giving exact equality for all axes. Consequently
\[
\boxed{\max_{X\succeq0,\,\operatorname{tr}X=1}\mathcal F(X)=3\sqrt2.}
\]

**Rank-one optimum:** Every rank-one unit-trace state \(X=zz^*\), \(\|z\|=1\), has a Bloch vector \(r\in\mathbb R^3\) with
\(r_a=z^*\sigma_a z\) and \(r_x^2+r_y^2+r_z^2=1\); conversely every real unit Bloch vector arises from some unit \(z\). Direct substitution gives
\[
\mathcal F(zz^*)=\sum_{a=x,y,z}\left[
\sqrt{\frac{1+r_a}{2}}+\sqrt{\frac{1-r_a}{2}}\right]
=\sum_{a=x,y,z}f(r_a^2),
\quad f(t)=\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1-t}},\quad 0\le t\le1.
\]
The function \(f\) is strictly concave on \((0,1)\) (differentiate twice, or compose the strictly concave nondecreasing square root with \(1+\sqrt{1-t}\)); by Jensen,
\[
\sum_af(r_a^2)\le3f\!\left(\frac{r_x^2+r_y^2+r_z^2}{3}\right)
=3\sqrt{1+\sqrt{\frac23}}.
\]
Equality is attained by a pure state with Bloch vector \((1,1,1)/\sqrt3\). Thus
\[
\boxed{
\max_{\|z\|=1}\mathcal F(zz^*)
=3\sqrt{1+\sqrt{2/3}}
\ <\ 3\sqrt2
=\max_{X\succeq0,\ \operatorname{tr}X=1}\mathcal F(X).
}
\]
The strict inequality follows because \(\sqrt{2/3}<1\).

This **proves that PSD lifting can fail to preserve the correct rank-one optimum** for some complex 2×2 positive semidefinite matrices and six independent budgets. It does **not** claim that this six-projector family is directly realizable as the special Gram matrices generated by rational-arc increment multipliers; that realization is an independent research question.
